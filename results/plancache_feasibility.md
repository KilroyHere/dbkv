# Test P (plan-cache feasibility), draft

Pre-registered in PLAN §2h (commit 357ff09). Generated by `harness/plancache/test_p.py`.

## Method

- BM25 (k1 1.2, b 0.75) over all earlier questions of the same stream, all tenants; tokens = lowercase alphabetic words, digits dropped. Hint = template of the top-1 earlier question (ties: most recent).
- Threshold: seed-1 streams r20/r50/r80 pooled; lowest threshold with non-repeat false-hint rate <= 10%.
- **Repeat definitions.** With 30 templates the strict definition (template seen anywhere earlier) cannot produce 0/20/50% streams: strict repeat rate is >= 80% over 150 questions and >= 90% over 300. The streams control a windowed rate (template seen in the previous 25 questions). Both are evaluated, each with its own threshold.
